Abandon Ship Drill / Latihan Meninggalkan Kapal (Checklist 023)
Demonstration of abandon ship includes the launch of lifeboat / liferaft under special ship conditions: sink / aground / burn / crash / Peragaan Meninggalkan Kapal termasuk peluncuran sekoci / liferaft dg kondisi kapal khusus : tenggelam / kandas / terbakar / tabrakan

Launch & maneuvers Lifeboat (PS / STB) are conducted quarterly by the related crew; The lifeboat area to the embarkation deck (PS / STB) & LifeRaft Simulation (PS / STB) is conducted monthly by related crew / Peluncuran & Manuver sekoci (PS/STB) tiap triwulan oleh crew terkait; Area sekoci sampai dek embarkasi (PS/STB) & Simulasi LifeRaft (PS/STB) setiap bulan oleh crew terkait.
Safety Equipment Demonstration Drill / Latihan Peragaan Peralatan Keselamatan (Checklist 024)
Demonstration includes LifeJacket, Resistant Clothes, Breathing Apparatus, etc. / Peragaan meliputi LifeJacket, Baju Tahan Api, Alat Bantu Pernafasan

Performed every 3 months, the schedule can be adjusted with the period that has been running on the ship / Dilaksanakan setiap 3 bulan, schedule bisa disesuaikan dg periode yg sudah berjalan di kapal
