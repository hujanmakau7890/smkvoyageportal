PT MENTARI MAS MULTIMODA
091-D WEEKLY CREW'S ACCOMODATION INSPECTION
INSPEKSI AKOMODASI AWAK KAPAL MINGGUAN
SWKY
Release: Rev.0
Instructions / Instruksi :
Weekly inspections of seafarer accommodation (incl. on-board recreational facilities) to be carried out on board ships by Master, to ensure that seafarer accommodation is clean, decently habitable and maintained in a good state of repair as stated in Safety Management Manual (SMM) TE-S11 Ship Board Maintenance Procedure, DMLC II/Section-8, MLC 2006/Reg.3.1.
Inspeksi mingguan untuk akomodasi awak kapal dilakukan di atas kapal oleh Nakhoda, untuk memastikan akomodasi bersih, layak huni, dan dipelihara dalam kondisi baik sebagaimana dinyatakan dalam Prosedur Pemeliharaan Kapal SMM TE-S11.
